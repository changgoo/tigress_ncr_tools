# Gas-column density probability distributions

This document defines the clean-suite one-point statistics generated by
`plot-suite-density-pdf`. The analysis uses the face-on (`theta0`) total-gas
column-density field `nH`. Corrupted `R8_8pc_NCR_row0000` is excluded, leaving
31 models sampled every 1 Myr from 0 through 600 Myr.

The same estimator can be applied to `nHI` and `ne_sq` with
`--quantity hi` or `--quantity em`; tracer-specific fields, histogram ranges,
and output names are documented in
[projected_species_analysis.md](projected_species_analysis.md).

## 1. Variables and weighting

For each map, define the area-normalized column-density ratio

\[
R(x,y,t) \equiv \frac{\Sigma(x,y,t)}{\langle\Sigma(t)\rangle_A},
\qquad
\delta \equiv R-1,
\qquad
s \equiv \ln R.
\]

Both stored PDFs are area weighted: every map pixel has equal weight. For bin
\([u_i^-,u_i^+)\) of width \(\Delta u_i\), the estimator is

\[
p(u_i,t) \equiv \frac{N_i(t)}{N_{\rm pix}\,\Delta u_i},
\]

where \(u\) is either \(\delta\) or \(s\). The normalization uses all pixels,
including values outside the stored histogram interval. Consequently, the
integral over the finite stored range records the captured probability and
can be slightly smaller than unity if a tail leaves the interval.

The defaults are 100 linear bins over
\(-1\leq\delta\leq30\) and \(-6\leq s\leq4\). These limits affect only the
histograms, not the width measurements.

## 2. Instantaneous widths

The standard deviations are calculated directly from all pixels in every map:

\[
\sigma_\delta(t) \equiv
\sqrt{\left\langle[\delta(t)-\langle\delta(t)\rangle_A]^2\right\rangle_A},
\]

\[
\sigma_s(t) \equiv
\sqrt{\left\langle[s(t)-\langle s(t)\rangle_A]^2\right\rangle_A}.
\]

For each model, the primary plotted point is the temporal median over
200--600 Myr. Its asymmetric bar spans the temporal 16th--84th percentiles.
The archive and CSV also store the arithmetic mean, population standard
deviation, and accepted-sample count. Model colors use the logarithmically
normalized 200--600 Myr mean SFR through `plasma`.

These primary widths are the square roots of population variances measured
directly from the pixels; they are not parameters of a Gaussian fit. The
separate `median_s_gaussian_sigma` value is the moment width of the
time-median \(s\)-PDF described in Section 3. For a skewed or multimodal PDF,
there is no reason for that overlay width to equal the temporal median of the
instantaneous direct widths.

The 2-by-3 correlation figure places \(\sigma_\delta\) and \(\sigma_s\) in its
two rows and uses the following three horizontal variables:

\[
\kappa \equiv \sqrt{2(2-q)}\,\Omega,
\qquad
\rho_* \equiv \frac{\Sigma_*}{2H_*},
\qquad
\langle\Sigma_{\rm SFR}\rangle_{200-600}.
\]

A second correlation figure compares both widths with the whole-domain,
mass-weighted characteristic speeds derived from each model's `*.whole.hst`
history:

\[
\sigma_i \equiv \sqrt{\frac{2K_i}{M}},
\qquad
c_{\rm th} \equiv \sqrt{\frac{P}{M}},
\qquad
v_{A,i} \equiv \sqrt{\frac{2{\rm ME}_i}{M}}.
\]

The azimuthal kinetic component uses the background-shear-subtracted
\(\delta K_2\). Each point is the 200--600 Myr temporal median in both
coordinates. Horizontal and vertical bars independently span the 16th--84th
percentiles of the speed and PDF width. Thus, the bars show temporal scatter
within each model rather than uncertainty on the median.

A separate derived-velocity figure uses

\[
\sigma_{\rm 3D} \equiv
\sqrt{\sigma_1^2+\sigma_2^2+\sigma_3^2},
\qquad
v_{A,{\rm 3D}} \equiv
\sqrt{v_{A,1}^2+v_{A,2}^2+v_{A,3}^2}.
\]

The thermal history diagnostic \(\sqrt{P/M}\) is identified with \(c_s\). The
ordinary and magnetic-pressure-corrected Mach diagnostics are

\[
\mathcal{M} \equiv \frac{\sigma_{\rm 3D}}{c_s},
\qquad
\beta \equiv \frac{2c_s^2}{v_{A,{\rm 3D}}^2},
\qquad
\mathcal{M}_{\rm corr} \equiv
\frac{\mathcal{M}}{\sqrt{1+1/\beta}}.
\]

All five intermediate quantities, including \(\beta\), are calculated at each
history timestamp. The plotted medians and percentile ranges are then measured
from those instantaneous series; they are not constructed from independently
averaged velocity components.

## 3. Median PDF and Gaussian fit

At every stored \(s\) bin, the code takes the temporal median of
\(p(s,t)\) over 200--600 Myr. It also records the pointwise 16th and 84th
percentiles. Because a pointwise median of normalized distributions need not
itself integrate to unity, the median PDF is renormalized over the stored
range before fitting its moments.

The displayed Gaussian is

\[
p_G(s) \equiv \frac{1}{\sqrt{2\pi}\,\sigma_G}
\exp\left[-\frac{(s-\mu_G)^2}{2\sigma_G^2}\right],
\]

with moment estimates

\[
\mu_G \equiv \int s\,p_{50}(s)\,ds,
\qquad
\sigma_G^2 \equiv \int (s-\mu_G)^2p_{50}(s)\,ds.
\]

A Gaussian distribution in \(s=\ln(\Sigma/\langle\Sigma\rangle)\) corresponds
to a log-normal distribution of \(\Sigma\). The fit allows \(\mu_G\) and
\(\sigma_G\) to vary independently; it does not impose the ideal-lognormal
constraint \(\mu_G=-\sigma_G^2/2\). Departures between the measured median PDF
and the dashed curve therefore remain visible.

## 4. Archive and generated products

The default output directory is `SUITE/density_pdf_theta0/`. Its numerical
archive, `density_pdfs.npz`, includes:

- `pdf_delta_area` and `pdf_s_area`, each with shape `(31,601,100)`;
- `std_delta_time` and `std_s_time`, each with shape `(31,601)`;
- bin edges and centers, actual and target times, mean column density, model
  order, SFR colors, and exclusion metadata;
- temporal medians, 16th and 84th percentiles, means, standard deviations, and
  counts for both instantaneous widths;
- the same temporal summaries for all seven mass-weighted characteristic
  speeds, stored with keys such as `sigma_x1_time_median` and
  `alfven_x3_time_percentile84`;
- temporal summaries of `sigma_3d`, `alfven_3d`, `plasma_beta`, `mach_3d`, and
  `mach_mhd`, along with scalar text fields recording their definitions;
- pointwise temporal PDF quantiles and the fitted Gaussian curves;
- \(\Omega\), \(\kappa\), \(\rho_*\), and \(q\) for each model.

The directory also contains:

- `density_pdf_widths.csv`: one row per clean model with width summaries,
  parameters, Gaussian moments, and averaging bounds;
- `density_pdf_widths_correlations.png`: the 2-by-3 width correlation figure;
- `density_pdf_widths_velocity_correlations.png`: the 2-by-7 comparison with
  three kinetic dispersions, thermal speed, and three Alfvén speeds;
- `density_pdf_widths_derived_velocity_correlations.png`: the 2-by-4 comparison
  with \(\sigma_{\rm 3D}\), \(v_{A,{\rm 3D}}\), \(\mathcal{M}\), and
  \(\mathcal{M}_{\rm corr}\);
- `density_pdf_time_median.png`: all-model median \(\delta\) and \(s\) PDFs,
  with dashed Gaussian curves in the \(s\) panel. Its displayed limits retain
  the union of median-PDF bins with density at least \(10^{-4}\), removing
  empty histogram tails without changing stored PDFs or fitted moments;
- `density_s_pdf_gaussian_fits.png`: an SFR-ranked 4-by-8 grid showing every
  model's median \(s\)-PDF, 16th--84th percentile envelope, and Gaussian fit.

Run the analysis with

```bash
plot-suite-density-pdf /tigress/changgoo/anvil/TIGRESS-NCR-suite --workers 4
```

Use `--overwrite` only when the source maps or PDF grid have changed.
